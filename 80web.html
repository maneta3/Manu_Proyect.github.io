Around the World in 80 Days by Manuel

Literature timeline of Fogg's journey.

The Journey

In 1872, Fogg bet with stuart that he can travel around the world in 80 days. With his servant Passepartout, he crosses Europe, Asia and America while being followed by Fix, who is a detective working to found the gentlemen thief.

October 2, 1872

London — The Bet

Fogg accepts a £20,000 bet at the Gentlemen's Club and leaves London with Passepartout.

October 3–5, 1872

Across Europe

Fogg and Passepartout travel through Paris, Turin and Brindisi.

October 9, 1872

Suez — Detective Fix

They arrive in Suez aboard the Mongolia. Detective Fix begins following Fogg, believing he is the bank robber.

Comic illustration: The Mongolia and Detective Fix
October 20, 1872

Bombay — India

Fogg and Passepartout arrive in Bombay and continue their journey by train.

October 22–24, 1872

The Elephant

The railway is unfinished, so they steal an elephant and rescue a princess of being burnt. and cross part of India.

October 23, 1872

Aouda

Fogg and Passepartout rescue Ms. Aouda. She joins them on their journey.

October 25, 1872

Calcutta

The travelers reach Calcutta and continue toward Hong Kong by ship.

November 6–8, 1872

Hong Kong

Fogg, Aouda and Passepartout face delays. Passepartout becomes separated from the others by a trap of Fix..

November 13–14, 1872

Yokohama

The travelers reach Japan and reunite with Passepartout.

December 3, 1872

San Francisco

They arrive in the United States and continue east by train.

December 11, 1872

New York

They reach New York but miss their ship. Fogg finds another way to cross the Atlantic.

December 21, 1872

London — The Finish

Fogg returns to London believing he has lost. However, he discovers that he has gained one day by travelling around the world from west to east.

The Route

London → Paris → Turin → Brindisi → Suez → Bombay → Calcutta → Hong Kong → Yokohama → San Francisco → New York → London
